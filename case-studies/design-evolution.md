# From observing a scene to moving through a workflow

I want the visual design to explain what Citadel connects and automates. The project has explored several concrete directions, each retained as a version of the same website.

| Recorded checkpoint | Design role |
| --- | --- |
| October 22–30 | Observatory and fictional evidence exploration |
| November 9 | Cira and restrained cinematic presentation |
| November 17 | Photographic observatory direction |
| November 18 — Retail 031 | Street, shop and sample pickup request |
| November 19 — Workflow 032 | Navigable integration workflow with an authored sample |
| November 19 — Connected work 033 | Spatial documents, restrained materials and clearer business context |
| November 20 — Inside the workflow 034 | Continuous camera travel through input, context and action spaces |

The retail version made one business process tangible: inspect a product and follow an inquiry to a confirmed sample pickup. The later main-site versions broadened the explanation to integration and automation across existing tools. The machinery metaphor in 032 gave way to readable spatial documents in 033, but those documents still positioned the viewer outside the workflow.

The current direction uses one continuous route. Input channels converge, context structures surround the camera, and outputs branch toward records, follow-ups and responses. Ordinary document navigation remains the organizing layer. This keeps the business explanation reachable while the scene establishes depth and progression.

Refinement reduced repetitive structures, glare and labels that crossed the reading column. A short heading and relocated navigation controls improved the narrow layouts. Reduced-motion and WebGL-fallback behavior preserve access when camera movement or rendering is unsuitable.

The saved designs document iteration, not customer adoption. Historical animation-boundary findings remain in the [retail verification](../verification/2026-11-18-retail.md); current checks and their limits are in the [workflow verification](../verification/2026-11-20-workflow.md).
